4 · Scale & measure

Standard scales: click one, or drag it onto a drawing to set that drawing's scale.
Real length mm
Ruler: press on a point and drag to the next one (or click the two points); the distance is shown at the scale you set. Points snap to line ends and corners. Calibrate: click the two ends of something you know the size of, such as the scale bar or a dimensioned wall.

5 · Rooms & space

Draw a room by clicking its corners (they snap to the walls). Click the first corner again, press Enter or double-click to close it. Needs a scale.

6 · Furniture & appliances

Drag an item onto the plan, or pick it and click where it goes. Drag to move, R to rotate, Delete to remove. The dashed zone in front is working / door clearance.

7 · Options & changes

Each option is its own set of furniture, so you can try "stove left" against "stove right". Items start as proposed; accept or reject them.

8 · Review pins & comments

Pins from the PDF's own notes are imported automatically. Drag a pin to the spot it refers to.

9 · Markup & notes

The drawing tools are in the bar above the plan: Pen, Arrow, Text and Eraser. With no tool chosen, click a mark to select, move or edit it. The eraser removes pen, arrow and text marks only; furniture is removed with Delete.

10 · Export & save

Name this version, then save an image to share or an editable file to reopen later.
The saved file contains the plan itself plus your rooms, furniture, text, pins, measurements and markup, so you can reopen it on its own (also from step 1 at the top) and carry on.
